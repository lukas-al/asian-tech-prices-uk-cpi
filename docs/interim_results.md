# Interim modelling results

This note has been superseded by the completed one-to-twelve-month analysis in
[`research_report.md`](research_report.md) and the concise senior-facing version
in [`killer_conclusion.md`](killer_conclusion.md).

The current assessment is:

- no country provides a stable general nine- or twelve-month signal for the
  preferred UK CPI aggregate excluding games;
- Korea remains most useful at short horizons, while China and Hong Kong show
  selected medium-horizon information for UK border prices;
- C261 import prices robustly improve headline UK technology-CPI forecasts at
  one to four months, but no border series provides a robust nine- or
  twelve-month retail signal;
- component evidence is concentrated in mobile phones, portable A/V,
  sound-and-vision, photographic/optical goods and computer accessories; and
- the four-country ridge result at three to eight months is promising but has
  only 12–21 forecast errors.

Data vintage: 2 August 2026.
